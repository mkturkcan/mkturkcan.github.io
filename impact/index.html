Sep 2026

Valdrada

A real-time digital twin of New York City built from public data

  • 140 GitHub stars in its first three weeks.
  • Its streamed-tile architecture is followed by Procedural Tokyo and, through it, a San Juan port. 3DStreet documented it as prior art, and Databook.nyc asked to link it from its building pages.
Apr 2026

UrbanOmniDetect

Calibration-free monocular 3D object detection

  • 4,458 downloads of the released model on Hugging Face.
  • Benchmarked at 30 frames per second on a laptop's integrated GPU by condados.ai, and ported to C++ and Qt as OmniBEV.
Mar 2026

DART

Real-time open-vocabulary object detection

  • 781 GitHub stars and 93 forks in its first seven months; the launch announcement drew more than 2,500 reactions on LinkedIn.
  • Proposed for integration into Intel's Open Edge Platform, with OpenVINO models, and into the OCTRON animal-tracking tool.
  • Benchmarked against DeepLabCut at the Lemanic Life Sciences Hackathon 2026, extended by DAIRT to detect interactions between objects, and listed in Awesome Segment Anything.
Feb 2026

NYC congestion pricing analysis

  • The Center for Smart Streetscapes runs the project's real-time camera map on its own site.

generative-agents

  • 994 GitHub stars and 163 forks.
