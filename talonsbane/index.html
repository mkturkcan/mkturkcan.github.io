The Game

Six stages Two campaigns

A dragon, a screen full of bullets, and an arcade cabinet built out of a CRT — curvature, shadow mask, and the reflection of the picture on the moulding.

Six hand-built stages, a boss at the end of each, and two campaigns that fly the same war from opposite sides. It runs entirely offline. It collects nothing.
