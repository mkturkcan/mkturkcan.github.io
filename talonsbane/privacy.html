Privacy Policy

Talonsbane Last updated 24 August 2026

Talonsbane does not collect any data about you.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page says it precisely, because “we don't collect data” is a sentence a lot of apps write while collecting data.

What the app sends

Nothing. Talonsbane makes no network requests of any kind. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party code in it. The game works identically with the device in airplane mode, because it never had anything to connect to.

What the app stores

One save file, on your device. It holds your progress: high scores, which campaigns you have finished, your chosen dragon and difficulty, and your settings. It is written to the app's own private storage, it never leaves your device, and no other app can read it.

Deleting Talonsbane deletes it. There is no copy anywhere else — which also means there is no way to recover it, so if you delete the app your scores are gone.

What the app asks permission for

Nothing. Talonsbane requests no permissions: no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no photos, no notifications, and no tracking. If your device ever prompts you for a permission on this app's behalf, something is wrong and you should not grant it.

Children

Talonsbane is rated 13+ for fantasy violence. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, so there is nothing for a parent to request, correct or delete — and nothing that could be disclosed to anyone else, because no data leaves the device in the first place.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything — it has no plans to — this page will say so before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
