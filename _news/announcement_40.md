Our preprint, <em>Dynamic Minimax Regret Optimization for Robust LLM Post-Training</em>, led by Chengbo Zang, is now on <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06329" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">arXiv</a>. Its DUCB-OGD algorithm pairs a discounted upper-confidence-bound sampler over data sources with online gradient descent, improving worst-group robustness across supervised fine-tuning, preference optimization, and reinforcement learning with negligible overhead.
