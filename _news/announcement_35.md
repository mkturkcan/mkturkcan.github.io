I released <a href="https://github.com/mkturkcan/boundless-nyc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">BoundlessNYC</a>, a city-scale digital twin of New York City compiled from public municipal records. It covers Manhattan, the Bronx, Brooklyn, and Queens with simulated traffic and pedestrians, pixel-exact segmentation and depth ground truth, and a CARLA-style Python API. An <a href="https://huggingface.co/spaces/mehmetkeremturkcan/boundless-nyc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">interactive demo</a> runs in the browser.
