I released <a href="https://github.com/mkturkcan/valdrada" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Valdrada</a>, a city-scale digital twin of New York City compiled from public municipal records. It covers Manhattan, the Bronx, Brooklyn, and Queens with simulated traffic and pedestrians, labels every frame with segmentation and depth, and has a Python API modeled on CARLA. An <a href="https://huggingface.co/spaces/mehmetkeremturkcan/valdrada" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">interactive demo</a> runs in the browser.
