Our paper, <em>Zero-Shot Image Tagging at Traffic Intersections via SAM-Guided Vision-Language Models</em>, led by William Ho, has been accepted to the <a href="https://www.icmla-conference.org/icmla26/maintrackregularpapers.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">main track</a> of <a href="https://www.icmla-conference.org/icmla26/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ICMLA 2026</a>. Without any in-domain training, its SAM-guided vision-language pipeline outperforms established zero-shot methods and a trained baseline, with the largest gains on rare, safety-critical classes.
