I released <a href="https://huggingface.co/mehmetkeremturkcan/StreetPrompt" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">StreetPrompt</a>, an open-vocabulary video classifier for street cameras. Users describe situations such as flooding, fog, or collisions in plain text, with no labeled data or retraining, and the models run on hardware from a Jetson Orin Nano to desktop GPUs. The <a href="https://huggingface.co/datasets/mehmetkeremturkcan/SyntheticStreetScenes" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Synthetic Street Scenes</a> training dataset is also public.
