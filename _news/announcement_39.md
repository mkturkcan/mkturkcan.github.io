I posted <em>Reinforcement Learning of Communication in a Mesh of Small Language Models</em> on <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30578" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">arXiv</a>. It introduces TalkMesh, a decentralized mesh of small language model agents that learns when and what to communicate. Three agents that together generate at most six outputs reach the accuracy of majority voting over 32 samples, and at 32 agents the mesh raises Qwen3.5-0.8B's GSM8K accuracy from 0.568 with self-consistency to 0.705.
