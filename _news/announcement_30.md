With Soham Samal and Zoran Kostic, I released <a href="https://huggingface.co/mehmetkeremturkcan/SAM3-Joint-Surgical" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Surgical SAM 3.1</a>, a text-prompted detector and instance segmenter for surgical instruments, anatomy, and tissue, fine-tuned from SAM 3.1 on four public surgical datasets. The <a href="https://huggingface.co/datasets/mehmetkeremturkcan/SAM3-Joint-Surgical-Dataset" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">accompanying dataset</a> is also available.
