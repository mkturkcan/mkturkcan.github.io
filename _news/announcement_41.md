Our paper, <em>Harnessing Floating Car Data, Traffic Camera Observations, and Network Flow Analysis for Traffic Volume Estimation</em>, led by Antonina Kosikova, is now published open access in <a href="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44290-026-00657-x" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Discover Civil Engineering</a>. It fuses floating car trajectories, municipal traffic camera observations, and network flow models to estimate and forecast traffic volumes across a Manhattan road network.
