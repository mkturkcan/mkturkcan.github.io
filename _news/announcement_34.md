I released <a href="https://github.com/mkturkcan/urbananonymizer" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UrbanAnonymizer</a>, a face and license plate anonymizer for street-level images and video. It outperforms EgoBlur Gen2 on the UFDD and PP4AV benchmarks, and offline tracking keeps masks in place in frames where detections drop out. The <a href="https://huggingface.co/mehmetkeremturkcan/UrbanAnonymizer" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">models</a> and <a href="https://huggingface.co/datasets/mehmetkeremturkcan/UrbanAnonymizer-Dataset" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">dataset</a> are on Hugging Face.
