Our paper, <em>Newell-Car-Following-Model-Informed Gaussian Process Regression Model for Ultra-Short-Term Arterial Vehicle Trajectory Prediction</em>, led by Noshin Saiyara Ahmad, has been accepted for publication in the <a href="https://journals.sagepub.com/home/trr" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Transportation Research Record</a>. It predicts vehicle trajectories two seconds ahead, giving intersection safety alerts time to absorb sensing, communication, and reaction delays. On data from the DataCity Smart Mobility Testing Ground in New Brunswick, NJ, informing Gaussian process regression with Newell’s car-following model lowers overall RMSE by 17.5% to 47.2% relative to LSTM and TrajNet++.
